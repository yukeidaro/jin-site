Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Jin AI collects on jinai.md, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@jinai.md.

Who we are

Jin AI operates jinai.md. You can reach us at hello@jinai.md.

What we collect

When you request a demo, we collect the details you type into the form: your work email, first and last name, job title, phone number if you choose to give it, company, industry, company size and country. We also record the time of your request and whether our reply email was sent.

When you visit the site, Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits without cookies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver the fonts.

How we use it

We use your demo request only to reply to you, set up a demo, and follow up about that request. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Who processes it for us

Google stores demo requests and sends our reply emails (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail). GitHub hosts the website. Cloudflare provides visit counts. These providers may process data outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep a demo request for as long as we need it to respond and follow up, and we delete it sooner if you ask.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you gave us, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@jinai.md and we will reply without undue delay. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.

Cookies and Do Not Track

jinai.md does not use cookies. We do not track you across other websites, so we do not respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
