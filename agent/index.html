Jin AI for agents

Direct answer

Jin AI is a context layer that learns how you work and briefs the AI tools you use, with Markdown as the spine. It is for founders and operators who use more than one AI every day and is currently in build.

Problem

You keep re-prompting AI, and it still misses what you mean. AI gets the first draft right, then drifts. People retype the same context, and writing instruction files for every AI has become a job of its own.

AI does not know which details must be exact and which it can decide. People compensate by repeating themselves in every chat and by writing instruction files such as CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and custom instructions, which they then have to keep up to date by hand. Each AI keeps its own memory, so the work starts over in every tool.

Evidence and limits

Nearly 40% of the time employees save with AI is lost to rework: correcting errors, rewriting content and verifying output.

Scope: Survey of 3,200 employees and business leaders, fielded by Hanover Research in November 2025. Not research on Jin AI users.

Planned product behavior

Jin AI learns how you work, then briefs your AI on every request. You should not have to write instructions for your AI. You should be able to read and fix the ones it uses.

Learn

Read the files and AI chat history already on your computer and work out how you work: what must be exact, and what the AI can decide.

Brief

With each request, give the AI only the memories that matter for that task, so the last answer is as good as the first.

Show

Keep everything it learned as plain Markdown, show which memories each AI received, and let you correct any of them.

Direct questions and answers

What is Jin AI?

Jin AI learns how you work from the files and AI chat history already on your computer, and gives that context to the AI tools you use, so you stop re-explaining yourself. What it learns is kept as plain Markdown you can read and fix.

Who is Jin AI for?

Founders and operators who use two or more AIs every day, know they should set up instructions such as CLAUDE.md or custom instructions, and never quite did, or gave up keeping them current.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude memory?

Built-in memory is a short list of facts that stays inside one AI. Jin AI learns from your files as well as your chats, keeps what it learns structured by what must be exact and what the AI can decide, and is designed to brief every AI you use.

Do I have to write CLAUDE.md or custom instructions?

No. Jin AI learns from what you already have. Keeping CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and copilot-instructions.md current for you is planned, not shipped.

Does Jin AI record my screen or upload my files?

Jin AI never records your screen. The free version learns from files and chat history on your own computer, and your files stay where they are. Syncing across devices is a planned, optional Pro feature.

Can I see and change what it remembers?

Yes. Everything Jin AI learns is plain Markdown. You can read it, search it, correct it, and see which memories each AI received for a request.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude Code first. Plugins for ChatGPT, Codex and GitHub Copilot are planned.

Is Jin AI available now?

Not publicly. The desktop app is in build and a first beta is opening in small batches. Join the waitlist to be considered.

How much will Jin AI cost?

Free on your own computer. Pro is proposed at $10 per month for Google Drive and Notion sources and memory across devices. Team plans start with a conversation. All prices are being tested in the beta.

Business model

Free on your computer. Pro follows you everywhere. Free: Jin AI learns from the files and AI chat history on your own computer. Pro, proposed at $10 per month: adds sources such as Google Drive and Notion, and memory synced across your computer and phone. Teams: we start with a conversation and shape the plan around what the team needs.

All prices are a proposal we are testing in the beta. Nothing is charged today.
