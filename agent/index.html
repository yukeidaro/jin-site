For AI-forward companies.

Problem

When your best AI users leave, you start over

  • Agents stop working: When the person who built them leaves, their agents lose the context they ran on.
  • Successor rebuilds from scratch: The output gets handed over. The context, rules and judgment behind it do not.
  • Best practice stays hidden: One team finds a workflow that works. The other teams never see it.

Solution

Building

Safe for every employee

Put your personal context in securely, and take it out safely. Also usable in your personal AI.

Reusable for the company

Keep confidential data in, see how each role uses AI, and hand the best harnesses to colleagues and successors.

Your company's context stays yours

  • Each company's context is kept separate.
  • Jin AI never records your screen.
  • Jin AI itself never trains on your data.
