Jin AI is a one-click AI setup for business professionals. It is for business professionals who use AI at work and is currently in build.

Problem

AI does not start with the context it needs. Your files, slides and chats contain the details that make an answer useful, but each AI starts without them.

Introducing yourself again, writing long prompts and finding the right files for every request take time. Jin AI is building a setup that brings the relevant context to your AI.

Direct questions and answers

What is Jin AI?

Jin AI gives you the AI setup engineers have. One click, and every AI learns about you. It is in build; support for more AI tools is planned.

Who is Jin AI for?

Business professionals who want their AI tools to know who they are, how they work and which files matter.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude memory?

Each AI's built-in memory stays in its own product. Jin AI is building one setup from your files, slides and chats that can work across AI tools. ChatGPT and Copilot integrations are planned.

What happens after setup?

Jin AI learns from your files, slides and chats, and fills in your context. The right files come to the right chats. These features are being built for the beta.

Does Jin AI record my screen or upload my files?

Jin AI never records your screen. The free version learns from files and chat history on your own computer. Jin AI doesn't move your files. Cloud Sync is planned for Pro.

Does Jin AI train on my data?

Jin AI itself never trains on your data. You choose what it reads, and what it shares; access controls are planned as the beta develops.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude Code is the first integration being built. ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot integrations are planned, not shipped.

Is Jin AI available now?

The app is in build and a beta is opening in small batches. Join the waitlist to show your interest.

How much will Jin AI cost?

The version that learns on your computer is free. Pro is proposed at $10 per month for planned Google Drive and Notion connections and sync between your PC and phone. The price is being tested in the beta.

Business model

Free on your computer. Cloud Sync is planned for Pro. The free version learns from files and chat history on your own computer. Pro, proposed at $10 per month, would add Google Drive and Notion connections and sync between your PC and phone. Team plans are under consideration.

All prices are a proposal we are testing in the beta. Nothing is charged today.
