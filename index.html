The problem

You keep re-prompting AI, and it still misses what you mean.

You ask AI to edit your proposal like a college-level English professor would. The first draft is great. Twenty minutes later the sentences start to fall apart, and you are typing it again.

AI gets you 80% of the way fast. The last 20% is where the time goes. In a Workday survey of 3,200 employees and business leaders, nearly 40% of the time people saved with AI was lost to rework: correcting errors, rewriting content and checking output.

Source: Workday, “Beyond Productivity: Measuring the Real Value of AI”, fielded by Hanover Research, November 2025. Not a study of Jin AI users.

YouEdit my proposal like a college-level English professor would. Keep my numbers exactly as they are.
AIHere’s a tighter version. I cut the opening to one sentence and moved your pricing table up, unchanged.
20 minutes and six requests later
AISure! Here’s a punchier take with bold headers, and I rounded the figures so they read more cleanly.
YouRemember, you’re a college-level English professor. And don’t touch the numbers.
Why it keeps happening

Making AI work for you is now a job of its own.

  • It misses your priorities.AI can’t tell what has to be exact from what it can just handle. So it rounds your numbers and keeps your clichés.
  • It’s new work.People now spend hours writing instructions and skills for AI, a job that didn’t exist before, and they rewrite them every week.
  • You start over in every AI.Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and ChatGPT each read their own instructions. What one learns about you never reaches the others.
The solution

From your files and chat history, Jin AI learns how you work.

You don’t write anything. Jin AI starts from what is already on your computer, and you only step in when something it learned is wrong.

  1. Learn
    What must be exact, and what AI can decide.Your proposals, notes and past chats show how you work: who wants the numbers first, which figures never get rounded, where tone doesn’t matter.
  2. Brief
    Only what matters, on every request.When you send a request, Jin AI quietly adds the few memories that fit that task. You don’t see it happen, and you don’t paste anything.
  3. Show
    Plain Markdown you can read and fix.Every memory is a short Markdown file. Jin AI shows which ones each AI received, and asks you to check the ones it isn’t sure about.
