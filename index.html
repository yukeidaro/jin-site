What both pages say, in order
  1. The gap: business documents are familiar but disconnected; linked notes are connected but built for power users
  2. The verdict: this is not a file-management problem — what's missing is an interaction layer
  3. The product: one workspace, three ways to look at it (Notebook, Focus, Project)
  4. The method: familiar on the surface, connected underneath, curated so only the human layer shows
  5. The architecture: JinAI sits under the tools, so anything that produces a document can feed it
  6. Why us: inside Microsoft's productivity ecosystem, and daily users of generative AI and connected notes
  7. Privacy: your documents stay where they already live
  8. Upcoming: what is being built, in what order
  9. Waitlist
The waitlist needs one line to go live Both pages ship with a working form. Until an endpoint is set it falls back to opening the visitor's mail client, so it is never a dead button. To make it collect properly, open either page and set the two constants near the bottom of the file:
WAITLIST_ENDPOINT — a Formspree, Tally or Basin URL that accepts a POST with an email field
WAITLIST_EMAIL — the address the mail fallback writes to
