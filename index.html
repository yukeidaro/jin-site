How it works

Jin learns how your team works.

  1. Connect your tools

    Jin connects to the apps your team already uses. Admins manage which tools and MCP servers employees connect, all in one place.

  2. Spot repetitive work

    Jin finds the tasks each employee does again and again.

  3. Get the work ready

    Jin doesn't just suggest an automation. It runs it in a sandbox and shows the finished output. Nothing changes until the employee approves it.

  4. Share what works

    Automations that work are offered to people doing similar work. Your company can also build organization skills that everyone can use.

Single control plane for what can be accessed.

Jin only accesses information your company has approved. Your employees can see what Jin used, review what it prepared, and decide what happens next.

It is never used to train AI models.
