How Jin AI keeps your AI

  • Keep it safe

    Separate confidential information from reusable context, skills, and workflows. Share each part only with the AI tools you approve.

  • Keep it in the company

    Context, instructions, tools, and workflows stay with the company, not with one person. Even when someone leaves, the setup keeps working and the next person can continue from there.

  • Keep it shared

    Useful workflows and skills can be shared across teams and recommended where they fit, so good AI practices spread instead of staying in one team.

As people improve the way they work with AI, Jin AI keeps those improvements available to the whole company.
