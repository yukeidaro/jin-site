Accessibility

Last updated: October 19, 2026

We want jinai.md to work for everyone. Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we do

Every form field has a label, the site can be used with a keyboard alone, focus is visible, and illustrations have text descriptions. Animations stop when your device asks for reduced motion.

Known limits

Some illustrations explain an idea through motion. Their text descriptions carry the same idea.

Report a problem

If something on jinai.md does not work for you, email hello@jinai.md. Tell us the page and what happened, and we will reply.
